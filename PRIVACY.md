# Kinetic Life OS Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期：2026-09-30

This policy explains how Kinetic Life OS handles information when you use the public Progressive Web App hosted at `https://daoyi2026.github.io/kinetic-life-os/`.

本政策说明您使用公开部署于 `https://daoyi2026.github.io/kinetic-life-os/` 的 Kinetic Life OS 渐进式网页应用时，相关信息如何被处理。

## 中文

### 1. 核心原则

Kinetic Life OS 是一个本地优先的个人工作台。应用不要求注册账号，没有开发者控制的后端数据库，也不会自动把您输入的工作台记录同步到云端。

### 2. 保存在本地的数据

您输入或更新的以下内容会保存在当前浏览器配置文件的本地存储中：

- 项目、目标、待办、重要日期和项目推进记录；
- 体重、饮食热量、饮水、心情、精力、饮食简记和运动记录；
- 灵感、微风指南和其他工作台内容；
- 界面语言、侧边栏状态等本地偏好。

主要工作台数据保存在浏览器本地存储键 `serene-personal-workspace-v1` 中。项目维护者无法通过应用读取这些本地记录。

### 3. 不进行的处理

当前版本：

- 不要求账号或登录；
- 不使用广告、分析 SDK 或第三方跟踪器；
- 不把工作台记录发送给项目维护者；
- 不进行自动云同步；
- 不出售或共享用户输入的工作台记录。

### 4. GitHub Pages 托管

公开版本由 GitHub Pages 托管。应用本身不接收您的工作台记录，但 GitHub 可能按照其隐私声明处理提供网站所必需的常规技术信息，例如 IP 地址、设备和浏览器信息、请求日期与时间以及页面访问记录。请参阅 [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)。

### 5. 导出、导入与删除

- 您可以在“设置与备份”中导出 JSON 备份；
- 您可以导入 JSON 备份，以替换当前浏览器中的工作台记录；
- 您可以在“设置与备份”中清除当前设备记录；
- 您也可以通过浏览器设置清除此网站的存储数据。

卸载 PWA 不一定会同时删除浏览器存储。若要删除记录，请优先使用应用内的“清除全部记录”，或在浏览器中清除该网站的数据。

### 6. 本地安全限制

应用不会对浏览器本地存储或导出的 JSON 文件额外加密。能够访问您的设备、浏览器配置文件或备份文件的人，可能读取其中的内容。请妥善保护设备和备份文件，不要在公开或共用设备中保存敏感记录，也不要输入密码、身份证件、支付信息或其他不必要的高度敏感信息。

### 7. 外部链接

应用或项目文档可能包含指向 GitHub 等外部网站的链接。只有在您主动打开这些链接时，对应网站的隐私政策才适用。

### 8. 政策更新

如果产品的数据处理方式发生实质变化，本政策会同步更新，并修改顶部的生效日期。

### 9. 联系方式

如有隐私问题，可通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/daoyi2026/kinetic-life-os/issues) 联系项目维护者。Issues 是公开区域，请勿在其中提交健康记录、联系方式或其他私人信息。

## English

### 1. Core principle

Kinetic Life OS is a local-first personal workbench. It does not require an account, has no maintainer-controlled backend database, and does not automatically sync your workbench records to the cloud.

### 2. Data stored locally

The following information that you enter or update is stored in the local storage of your current browser profile:

- Projects, goals, tasks, important dates, and project updates;
- Weight, calorie intake, hydration, mood, energy, food notes, and workout records;
- Inspirations, Breeze guide entries, and other workbench content;
- Local preferences such as interface language and sidebar state.

The main workbench data is stored under the browser local-storage key `serene-personal-workspace-v1`. The project maintainer cannot access these local records through the app.

### 3. What the app does not do

The current version:

- Does not require an account or sign-in;
- Does not use advertising, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers;
- Does not send workbench records to the project maintainer;
- Does not provide automatic cloud synchronization;
- Does not sell or share user-entered workbench records.

### 4. GitHub Pages hosting

The public version is hosted by GitHub Pages. The app itself does not receive your workbench records, but GitHub may process ordinary technical information needed to provide the website, such as IP address, device and browser information, request date and time, and page usage data, under the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### 5. Export, import, and deletion

- You can export a JSON backup from Settings & backup;
- You can import a JSON backup to replace the workbench records in the current browser;
- You can clear current-device records from Settings & backup;
- You can also clear this website's stored data through your browser settings.

Uninstalling the PWA may not remove its browser storage. To delete records, use “Clear all records” in the app or clear the site's data in your browser.

### 6. Local security limitations

The app does not add encryption to browser local storage or exported JSON files. Anyone with access to your device, browser profile, or backup file may be able to read its contents. Protect your device and backups, avoid storing sensitive records on public or shared devices, and do not enter passwords, identity documents, payment information, or other unnecessary highly sensitive information.

### 7. External links

The app or project documentation may link to external websites such as GitHub. The destination site's privacy policy applies only when you choose to open one of those links.

### 8. Policy updates

If the product's data-handling practices change materially, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

### 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/daoyi2026/kinetic-life-os/issues). Issues are public, so do not include health records, contact details, or other private information.
